# Nordlysdiadem / Aurora diadem

Ny original PNG generert med det innebygde bildeverktøyet, én generering. Gjeldende prosjektfil: dist/characters/hat-aurora.png (1254×1254 RGBA med ekte transparens). Ingen eksisterende figurer eller plagg er erstattet.

## Prompt brukt
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent PNG fantasy mobile-game wearable headwear, a single standalone Aurora diadem for Glimbraid / Nordlysvev.
Primary request: Create a NEW elegant aurora diadem with a curved delicate silver-gold circlet, luminous mint and violet aurora crystal arcs. Refined, slightly more prestigious than an amber crown, but restrained.
Style references: the two displayed Glimbraid hats are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Match their polished hand-painted family-friendly fantasy-game aesthetic, softly glowing faceted translucent crystals, slender curling leaf-like precious-metal settings, warm edge highlights, readable silhouette at small avatar scale.
Subject: ONE diadem with a gently bowed horizontal band, short curling sides, a central mint-violet luminous crystal arc and smaller balanced aurora crystal arcs that sweep upward gracefully like northern-light ribbons, with delicate silver and pale gold filigree. Restrained detail and moderate height.
Composition/framing: square canvas; front view with a very slight view of the curved wearable band interior, symmetrical and centered; horizontal crown occupies about 88% of canvas width and 55% of canvas height, centered at y=45%, generous empty transparent margins. Clean standalone asset for overlay on small avatar heads.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background, preserve alpha including soft luminous edges.
Constraints: no head, face, mannequin, person, support, pedestal, extra objects, text, lettering, watermark, border, cast shadow or painted background. Exactly one item.
